Job Discovery Mail

A personal tool for organizing job opportunities from a dedicated Gmail mailbox and public employer career pages.

What it does

Job Discovery Mail extracts job titles, employers, listing links and source context, checks supported public postings, and keeps a private record for the account owner to review. It does not apply for jobs or contact employers.

Why Gmail access is needed

The owner authorizes Gmail read-only access to a dedicated jobs account. The collector processes messages within its collection window across that mailbox, including Spam and Trash, to find newsletters and job alerts. It does not send, edit or delete mail.

Personal use only

This application is operated by its owner for their own job discovery. It is not a public signup service. This website explains the application; it does not display private job records or request Gmail authorization.
